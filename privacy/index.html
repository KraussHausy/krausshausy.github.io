Krauss Kuts Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Krauss Kuts ("we", "us") collects when you book an appointment on our demo site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Booking details: your name, phone number, email address, and the service, time and barber you choose.
  • Visit history: past appointments and notes such as preferred cut.
  • Device data: basic browser and device information used to keep the site working.

How we use it

  • To book, confirm and remind you of appointments.
  • To contact you about changes to your visit.
  • To send offers, only if you opt in.
  • To improve the site.

Text messages and email

We send appointment confirmations and reminders by text or email. Marketing messages are sent only with your consent. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or use the unsubscribe link in any email.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers who help us run bookings and messaging, and when the law requires it.

Retention

We keep booking records for as long as you remain a client, then delete or anonymize them within a reasonable period.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or opt out of marketing, by contacting us at the address below.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date at the top of this page.
